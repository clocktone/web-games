Privacy Policy

Pop It Bubble Blast — Last updated: August 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Pop It Bubble Blast (the "App"), published by Clocktone ("we", "us"). We built the App to be played entirely offline, and we designed it to collect no data at all.

Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or any other data. Specifically, the App does not:

  • collect personal information (name, email, address, phone number, or identifiers);
  • collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data;
  • use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs;
  • make network connections — the App works fully offline;
  • require or create user accounts.

Game Progress

Your game progress (such as completed levels and scores) is stored only locally on your device. It never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

Third Parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services. No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

Children

Because the App collects no data from anyone, it also collects no data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App changes any of the above (for example, by adding analytics or advertising), we will update this policy before that version is released and note the new date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@clocktone.io
